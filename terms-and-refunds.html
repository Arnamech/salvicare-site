1. Orders & Payment

Orders placed through this page can be paid via Cash on Delivery (COD), GCash, bank transfer, or online payment through PayMongo. Prices shown are in Philippine Pesos (₱) and include applicable taxes unless stated otherwise.

2. Shipping & Delivery

Orders are shipped nationwide via J&T Express or LBC (or, for select same-day Metro Manila orders, a courier partner such as Lalamove/Grab). Estimated delivery time is 2–7 business days depending on location. Free shipping applies automatically on orders of 2 or more bottles. Once a parcel is handed to our courier partner, delivery timing and physical handling are governed by that courier's own service terms; see Section 3 for what to do if a parcel arrives damaged.

3. Delivery Inspection & Damage Claims

Please inspect your parcel at the time of delivery, before signing or accepting it as complete.

  • If the outer packaging or the product itself appears damaged, tampered with, or incorrect, report it to us within 48 hours of delivery.
  • Your report must include clear photos (or a short video) of the parcel packaging as received and the product inside, before you dispose of any packaging materials.
  • Claims reported after 48 hours, or without photo/video evidence, may be declined — we're unable to verify what happened to a parcel we can no longer see.

4. Refund & Replacement Policy

All refund and replacement requests require photo (or video) evidence of the product submitted to us before you send anything back or we approve any refund. This lets us resolve most cases without asking you to physically return a consumable health product.

(a) Change of mind / not satisfied with results — our 60-Day Guarantee

Covered only while the bottle's safety seal is intact and the product is unopened, claimed within 60 days of delivery. Once a bottle has been opened, it is no longer eligible for a change-of-mind refund — this is a consumable food supplement, and once the seal is broken we can no longer verify its condition or resell/redistribute it. This limit exists for hygiene and product-safety reasons consistent with FDA-regulated food product handling.

(b) Damaged, defective, or wrong item shipped

Covered regardless of whether the product has been opened, provided you report it within the 48-hour window in Section 3 with clear photos/video. We do not require you to mail back an opened product for this category — photo/video evidence is sufficient for us to process a replacement or refund.

To claim either category:

  • Message us on Facebook or through the contact details on the order page, with your order reference/tracking number, reason for the request, and required photos/video.
  • Approved refunds are processed to the original payment method (or via GCash/bank transfer for COD orders) within 7–14 business days.
  • Decisions on borderline cases (e.g. partial use, delayed reporting) are made at I2M2 Trading's reasonable discretion.
