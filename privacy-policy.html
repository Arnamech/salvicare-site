Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 7, 2026

I2M2 Trading ("we," "us," "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you order Salvicare Capsule, Fiber-C, or Salvicare Coffee through our website or Facebook page, and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

  • Name, phone number, and delivery address — needed to process and deliver your order.
  • Payment information — processed directly by PayMongo (our payment processor) or collected on delivery for Cash on Delivery (COD) orders. We do not store your card details.
  • Order history and communication — messages sent via Facebook, SMS, or WhatsApp regarding your order.
  • Photos or videos you submit for a damage, defect, or refund claim (see our Terms & Refund Policy) — used only to review that claim.
  • Newsletter/perks sign-up details, if you sign up separately from placing an order (name, phone, and/or email).
  • Reviews, testimonials, or feedback you send us about our products.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process, fulfill, and deliver your order.
  • To send order confirmations, tracking updates, and delivery notifications.
  • To review and resolve damage/defect/refund claims using the photos or videos you submit.

3. Marketing Consent — Campaigns, Newsletter & Perks

We only send marketing messages (health tips, product updates, promos, newsletter content, or perks/rewards) if you've opted in — for example, by checking a consent box at checkout, or signing up separately for our newsletter or perks program.

  • If you checked the campaign/marketing consent box, we'll send you SMS, email, and/or Facebook Messenger updates beyond your order status.
  • If you did not check that box, you'll only receive transactional messages about your own order (confirmation, tracking, delivery) — nothing else.
  • You can opt out of marketing messages anytime by replying "STOP" to any SMS, unsubscribing from the newsletter, or messaging us directly. Opting out does not affect transactional messages about an active order.

4. Customer Reviews & Testimonials

If you send us a review, testimonial, comment, or feedback (via Facebook, SMS, email, or any other channel), you give us permission to feature it — in full or in part, with your first name/initials and, if you shared one, your photo — on our website, social media pages, and marketing materials. If you'd prefer we not use your review publicly, let us know when you send it, or contact us afterward and we'll take it down.

5. Who We Share Your Information With

  • Our payment processor (PayMongo) — to process your online payment.
  • Our delivery partners (J&T Express, LBC, or a courier such as Lalamove/Grab for local same-day orders) — solely to deliver your package.
  • Our CRM system (GoHighLevel) — to manage your order status, marketing consent preferences, and respond to inquiries.

We do not sell your personal information to any third party.

6. Data Retention & Security

We retain order and contact information for as long as needed to fulfill orders, handle warranty/guarantee claims, and comply with tax/accounting requirements. We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data from unauthorized access.

7. Your Rights

Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012, you have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data, and to withdraw marketing consent at any time. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below. As an online seller, we are also subject to the Internet Transactions Act (RA 11967, 2023) and its consumer redress requirements.
